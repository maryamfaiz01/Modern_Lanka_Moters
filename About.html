Who We Are

Sri Lanka's Trusted Source for Genuine Spare Parts

Welcome to Modern Lanka Motors, your trusted supplier of high-quality automotive spare parts. Located in Gampola, Sri Lanka, we serve vehicle owners across the island with genuine OEM and premium aftermarket parts.

Our Journey

Our Story

Modern Lanka Motors began as a small family business in 2012, born from a passion for helping Sri Lankan vehicle owners find the right parts at fair prices. Over the years, we grew from a modest shop in Gampola into one of the most trusted spare parts suppliers in the Central Province.

Our focus has always remained the same: delivering high-quality, genuine parts and ensuring every customer leaves satisfied. Today we stock over 200 products across 8 categories, and continue to grow — but our commitment to quality, honesty, and service never changes.
